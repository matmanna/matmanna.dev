NestEgg is a financial assistant you can call. There's no app to download and no password to remember: an ElevenLabs agent named Robin answers on a Twilio phone line, reads your balance in plain English, applies confirmed budget changes, and places proactive outbound alert calls when a bill is due or an unusual charge appears. Plaid surfaces balances and recurring transactions, while Gemini reviews transactions for fraud patterns.

Built at SteelHacks XIII with a caretaker dashboard for family, NestEgg reached 2nd place in the ElevenLabs "Out Loud!" challenge.
