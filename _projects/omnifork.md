Omnifork is a forge-neutral social-coding control plane: one frontend for people and projects spread across GitHub, GitLab, Tangled, Forgejo, Gitea, Codeberg, Bitbucket and SourceHut. It isn't another forge; each connected forge stays the source of truth, while Omnifork indexes portable information, adds a social layer you control, and routes actions back to the right host.

Connect and verify your accounts, see projects and activity together, follow people and projects across forges, curate lists and alerts, and find mirrors and duplicate projects.
