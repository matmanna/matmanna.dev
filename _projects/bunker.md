The Bunker is the internal platform for Builders Collective at Penn State, where I'm an engineer. Members sign in with Discord or email to apply, check in to events, track funding and paperwork, and import their hackathon projects from Devpost into recaps. Organizers get attendance codes, a live display, judging rounds and a leaderboard.
