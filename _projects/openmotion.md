openmotion makes short vertical videos (such as "day one of building X" clips and launch videos) together with Claude. You describe the video, record your own voice, drop in screenshots, GIFs and sounds, and leave timestamped comments on any subtitle, visual, shot or frame. Claude edits the timeline and the preview updates, with every change saved as a version you can go back to.

Style rules Claude learns from your feedback are saved to a profile and applied to every future video of that type, and reusable motions like slide-ins, pans and punch-in zooms are saved as edit types.
