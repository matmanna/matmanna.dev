Pointmaxx is a personal agent for credit card rewards. It looks at the store you're about to pay, checks the cards you carry, and tells you which one earns the most, through a Chrome extension at checkout, a text agent you can message from a store, and a web wallet.

A card's bonus depends on how the store codes the charge, not on what you buy, so "use the grocery card for groceries" is often wrong. Pointmaxx reasons about merchant codes on your device: it never asks for a card number, never reads your purchase history, and shows every outbound request before it happens.

Built at the National Renaissance Hackathon at super{set}.
