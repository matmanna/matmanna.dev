BetterBill checks a hospital bill line by line. Snap a photo or paste the text, confirm each line, and BetterBill flags duplicates, CMS unit-limit errors, mismatches with your insurance statement, and charges above the hospital's own published cash price, with a source behind every amount.

It also screens for financial assistance under the hospital's policy and federal and state rules, using Browser Use to find a missing policy live. Once you approve the exact text, it sends a dispute letter and an ElevenLabs advocate calls billing for you. AI does the reading, writing, talking and browsing; deterministic code does the math.

On a $12,000 sample bill, BetterBill finds $1,916.85 in questionable charges in about a second. Built at the LA Hacks AI Hackathon 2026, where it won the Visa "Future of Shopping" track and Photon's Best Voice Agent prize.
