### OS: Arch BTW

One day I will switch to NixOS. At least that's what I keep telling myself.

### Terminal Emulator: Ghostty (Zsh)

Been pretty happy with Zsh, Oh My Zsh, and Ghostty for a while now (since Deceember 2025)

### Multiplexer: Herdr

Been using herdr since July 2026 and before that I used... no multiplexer! Herdr was really the first thing that felt useful enough to use

### Text editor: Neovim (Lazyvim), VSCodium

For about 6 months between December 2025 and June 2026, I used VSCodium. Before that, I had used Neovim for about a year. Now I'm back to Lazyvim

### Version Control: Jujutsu, GitHub, and Tangled

When possible I mirror things to GitHub and Tangled. I'd prefer to use Tangled but my social community isn't quite established there. Also, I've been playing around with JJ on side-projects for a bit and I do really like it!

### Browser: Helium and Fennec

Helium on Desktop, Fennec on Mobile. It's really that simple!
